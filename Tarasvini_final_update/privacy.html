Privacy policy

Tarasvini is an online women's ethnic wear store based in Chennai, India. This page explains what information we collect through this website and our order process, and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Contact form: your name, phone or WhatsApp number and your message.
  • Reviews: your name, rating, review, and an optional photo or video link. With your agreement, these are shown on our reviews page.
  • Orders: when you order on WhatsApp or Instagram, the details needed to deliver your order, such as your name, phone number and delivery address, plus payment confirmation.

How we use it

  • To reply to your messages and process, deliver and support your orders.
  • To show reviews you have agreed to publish.
  • To let you know about new arrivals or offers, only if you have asked to receive them.

We do not sell your information.

Services we use

We share only what is needed with the services that run our website and deliveries: Netlify (website hosting and forms), Google (catalogue sheets and fonts), Cloudinary (product photos), WhatsApp and Instagram (messages and orders) and courier partners (delivery). Each has its own privacy policy.

Browser storage

This website stores a small setting in your browser so the "How to order" guide is not shown again on every visit. It does not identify you.

Your choices

To ask what information we hold about you, or to have your review or details removed, message us on WhatsApp or use our contact form.
